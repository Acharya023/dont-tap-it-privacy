Don't Tap It — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Don't Tap It, a mobile game published by Harsh Adhikari, handles information when you use the game.

1. Information stored by the game

Don't Tap It does not require an account, registration, or login. Game settings and best scores are stored locally on your device. The game does not directly ask you to provide your name, phone number, address, contacts, photos, or other personal information.

2. Advertising

Don't Tap It uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including interstitial and rewarded advertisements. Google Mobile Ads may collect and use information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, and information about ad interactions in accordance with Google's policies and the choices available to you.

For more information about how Google handles information in connection with advertising, please see Google's Privacy Policy and information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Analytics and tracking

Don't Tap It does not include a separate analytics or account-tracking system operated by the developer. Advertising-related data processing may occur through Google AdMob as described above.

4. Children's privacy

Don't Tap It is not specifically directed at children. The app does not knowingly request personal information from children. Advertising services may apply their own policies and safeguards regarding users and advertising.

5. Third-party services

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), a third-party service provided by Google. Your use of the game may therefore be subject to Google's policies in addition to this Privacy Policy.

6. Data security

We take reasonable steps to avoid collecting unnecessary personal information directly through the game. Information processed by third-party services is handled according to those services' own security and privacy practices.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game, its services, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
